# Acme Travel and Expense Policy

This policy applies to all Acme employees and contractors who travel on company business. It was last reviewed in March 2026 by the Finance team.

## Booking travel

All flights and rail journeys must be booked through the corporate travel portal at least 14 days before departure where possible. Economy class is the default for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy may be booked for flights longer than 6 hours with line-manager approval.

## Hotels

The nightly hotel limit is 180 GBP in London and 120 GBP elsewhere in the UK. International hotel limits are published by Finance for each city. Employees should choose hotels within walking distance of the client site when practical.

## Per diem and meals

The daily meal allowance (per diem) is 45 GBP for domestic travel and 60 GBP for international travel. Alcohol cannot be claimed as an expense under any circumstances. Client entertainment must be pre-approved by a director.

## Receipts and claims

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the end of the trip. Itemised receipts are required for every expense above 10 GBP. Claims submitted later than 90 days will be rejected unless the CFO grants an exception.

## Mileage

Employees using a personal car for business travel can claim 45 pence per mile for the first 10,000 miles in a tax year and 25 pence per mile after that.
