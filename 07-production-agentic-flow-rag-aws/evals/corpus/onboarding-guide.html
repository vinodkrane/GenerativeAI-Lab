Welcome to Acme

Your first week is designed to help you meet your team and set up your tools.

Laptop and accounts

IT will ship your laptop to your home address two days before your start date. On your first morning, sign in with the temporary password sent to your personal email and enrol a hardware security key for multi-factor authentication.

Probation and reviews

New starters have a probation period of six months. Your manager will hold check-ins at the end of month one and month three, and a formal probation review in month six.

Holiday allowance

Full-time employees receive 27 days of annual leave plus UK bank holidays. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over into the next holiday year.
