Acme Board Meeting Minutes - Q3 2026

The board approved the acquisition of Brightline Analytics for 42 million GBP, subject to regulatory clearance. The deal is expected to close in January 2027.

The board discussed a planned restructuring of the European sales organisation. Approximately 60 roles may be affected. Communication to staff is embargoed until the formal consultation begins.

The CFO reported that Q3 revenue was 118 million GBP, 9 percent above plan.
