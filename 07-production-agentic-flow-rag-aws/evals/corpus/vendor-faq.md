# Vendor Payments FAQ

## Payment terms

Acme pays approved supplier invoices within 30 days of receipt. Invoices must quote a valid purchase order number, otherwise they are returned to the supplier.

## Changing bank details

Suppliers who need to change their bank details must submit the request on company letterhead, and Acme Finance will verify the change by calling the supplier's registered phone number before any payment is made.
